# The 48-vessel fleet

Each traffic ship honors a physicist from history. **USS** marks physicists with U.S. teaching or research affiliations, including visiting posts and wartime research. The others use **Starship**. The prefix describes an affiliation, not nationality; honorary degrees and lecture tours alone do not determine it. Representative U.S. affiliations are recorded below.

The two outer orbiters are USS Weinberg at r = 10L and USS Hawking at r = 20L. Your pilot’s rocket is separate from this fleet.
